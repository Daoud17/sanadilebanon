About Sanadi Lebanon

Supporting Mental Health, Wellbeing & Community Care

Sanadi Association for Mental Health and Wellbeing is a Lebanese organization dedicated to promoting mental health awareness, strengthening community support, and contributing to a more inclusive and supportive environment for individuals and families.

Mission and Vision

Mission

To ensure that mental health and access to care is positioned as a basic human right that must be met for all persons, through awareness, advocacy, and dignified mental health services across the spectrum of care.

Vision

All persons in Lebanon pursue a better mental health and can reach dignified access to care without social, personal and structural barriers.

Core Values

Respect

To consider all individuals worthy of high regards and respect.

Integrity

To adhere to the highest moral and ethical principles in fulfilling Sanadi’s mission.

Compassion

To understand another person’s condition from their perspective and strive to respond to their needs.

Inclusiveness

To actively include stakeholders in decision making and the implementation of Sanadi’s strategic goals.

Accountability

To acknowledge and assume responsibility for all actions and decisions undertaken within the organization.

Collaboration

To actively seek partners and engage them in fulfilling Sanadi’s mission and vision.

Courage

To face challenges head-on, advocating for mental health despite potential obstacles or resistance.

Agility

To respond rapidly and effectively to change, adapting strategies and actions to meet evolving challenges and opportunities.
